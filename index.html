Common questions

Before you reach out

Answers to the questions homeowners ask most before their first call.

Is A&H a licensed contractor?

Yes. A&H Construction holds an active California CSLB license. The number appears on every page of this site and can be verified at cslb.ca.gov.

How do estimates work?

After a site visit, you receive a written scope and pricing. Ranges shared before a visit are rough and change once the space and selections are known.

Do you handle permits?

For work that requires permits, A&H coordinates the permit process and inspections as part of the project.

Can I speak with past clients?

References are available upon request and shared privately, subject to prior client permission and project relevance.
